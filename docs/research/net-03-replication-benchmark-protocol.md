# NET-03 — Authoritative Replication, Interest, Delta, Privacy and 8/16/32/64-Client Benchmark Protocol

**P00 PROTOCOL ONLY.** No engine/client/server binary, 8/16/32/64 players, real packet capture, CPU/GPU, measured throughput or runtime test. N03-T001..N03-T128 all FUTURE/NOT EXECUTED. Expected budgets, supported max clients and numerical p99 thresholds unselected and not owner approved.

## 1. Falsifiable hypotheses
H1: at least two properly authenticated clients receive per-recipient **authorized-only** immutable snapshots of NET-02 committed world, with non-public fields absent, regardless of distance/transport. H2: stable generational entity IDs and application ACKed same-recipient baselines let lossy datagrams eventually converge with bounded memory, while stale updates and misrouted sessions fail closed. H3: interest broadphase over WORLD's authoritative index plus per-recipient policy and priority reduces aggregate serialized bytes/CPU at realistic spatial density without starving critical lifecycle. H4: quantization/delta compression can reduce bytes at selected fidelity with acceptable CPU and no confidentiality regression. H5: scaling 8/16/32/64 synthetic clients is measurable and policy-correct; **does not preassert capacity**.

## 4. Measurement and benchmarking discipline
1. **Security gate first:** under each synthetic client-count axis test PUBLIC/OWNER_ONLY/TEAM_ONLY/SERVER_ONLY, opposing team, spectator valid/invalid and fog hidden entities. Parse actual generated packet after client decode and assert disallowed fields, identifiers, counts and coordinates *not present* where classified secret. Inspect wrong-principal cache reuse, delayed queue, revocation while unreliable payload queued and resume across session/scene migrations. A "server filters correctly" log is insufficient.
2. **Frozen same-source world:** authoritative NET-02 server changes only from GAME-04 accepted actions at CORE-04 TickId; WORLD-01 read barrier stable. NET-03 is observer, and compare same build/seed deterministic projection hashes; cross-platform bitwise equality not claimed.
3. **Loss and ACK:** model RTT 1/20/60/120 ms, jitter 0/5/20/50 ms and 0/1/5/10% loss incl burst and reordering in isolated virtual-network emulator. Distinguish NET-01 transport ACK vs client baseline-applied ACK, validate recovery after lost keyframes and revoked field, never use retransmit of forbidden state. Tool/host networking impairment only in authorized isolated environment, no public unsolicited traffic.
4. **CPU decomposition:** source-changes scan (WORLD), shared public-cohort quantization, per-peer field auth/projection, spatial query/hysteresis, scheduling, delta+codec encoding, NET-01 enqueue, client decode and present. Report total and per-client CPU, max/mean queue depth, RSS/heap peak and cache cardinality; separation enables finding "shared global state cache" hazards.
5. **Bytes and staleness:** snapshot payload vs transport framing+encryption/headers/PMTU; packets/s, bytes/s and drops/expired, p50/p95/p99 time since last authorized state at client for priority classes, lifecycle updates/resync, fairness/starvation and changed-field loss. Compare baseline full vs delta vs compression including ABI/CPU/security, not only ratio.
6. **Fairness:** use repeatable profiles for clients in dense vs sparse locations, near vs far, mobile vs stationary, packet loss asymmetry; enforce min/control budget and no hero "fast player" starvation. Under overload no private data leak; backpressure may delay cosmetic position but not fabricate committed gameplay state.
7. **Statistical proof:** warmup and repeated trials, raw samples sufficient for p99 (not 10 samples), independent seeds/hardware tiers, confidence intervals and failure logs. Record cold/warm connection/schema reset, CPU/thermal and NIC offloads; do not compare consumer LAN and cloud raw RTT as if equal.
8. **Native/Web capability:** if WebTransport DATAGRAM unsupported, WSS reliable-only limitation explicitly reported; real Windows/Linux/macOS/Web clients require actual builds/browser versions, not design claims. Preserve no mandatory paid service or instrumentation SaaS.
9. **Stopping:** HIGH/CRITICAL authority/privacy breach, invalid baseline and unbounded allocation, unsafe compressed secret side-channel, third-party license incompatibility or incorrect DoD => future BLOCKED/CORRECTION REQUIRED regardless of throughput. No 8/16/32/64 "PASS" until raw evidence.
