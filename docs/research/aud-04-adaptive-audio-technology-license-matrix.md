## Architecture/technology selection proposal
1. **Keep a first-party Stormline semantic model** for music program, tempo map, transition rules, dialogue line/event, voice playback, timed-text cue, locale resolution, cinematic cue, ducking policy, accessibility mix and receipts. Keep all public APIs vendor-neutral and versioned.
2. **Use AUD-01 sample frames as live playback truth**, CORE-04 TickId only as simulation intent and TWO-03 rational time as cinematic authoring truth. Map between them with explicit epoch/revision/latency receipts; never use graphics FPS or wall-clock seconds.
3. **Reuse AUD-03** for sidechain/ducking DSP, automation, snapshots, latency and parameter smoothing. AUD-04 owns policy and event timing, not compressor math or bus summation.
4. **Treat OTIO as optional import/export**, WebVTT/TTML as timed-text interchange, CLDR/BCP-47 as locale semantics. Use restricted adapters and DATA-03/04/05 staging. Do not replace the Stormline-owned TWO-03 timeline or DATA-04 canonical source.
5. **Use FMOD/Wwise/Godot as behavior references only.** Their transition matrix, beat quantization, dialogue key/localized bank, snapshots and profiling demonstrate useful patterns; no vendor binary/code/asset is approved.
6. **License review must be per layer:** code/library, middleware SDK/runtime, music composition, master recording, performer/voice likeness, translation, captions/SDH, sample/stem library, sound effect and synthetic voice. A permissive software license does not clear the audio content.
7. **Fallback and accessibility are contractual:** exact locale first, visible configured fallback or text-only, no silent wrong-language substitution; captions/SDH/audio description remain independent; critical gameplay cues are protected from ducking.
8. **Future comparison is evidence-driven:** transition sample accuracy, crossfade discontinuity, dialogue/caption sync, CPU/voice/memory/streaming, p95/p99 callback budget, platform support and asset rights must be tested against original legal fixtures.
