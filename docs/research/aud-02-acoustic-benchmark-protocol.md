# AUD-02 — Reproducible Spatial Acoustic & Binaural Benchmark Protocol

**Date:** 2026-11-14 | Research #198 | WO NLE-P00-WO-002-AUD-02 | **FUTURE ONLY, ZERO ACTUAL MEASUREMENTS.**

## Questions and goals
How many moving emitters and listeners can an engine-owned 3D spatial stage provide under measured AUD-01 callback p95/p99 latency, CPU heap budget and 0 false source/memory/privacy leaks? Does HRTF render correct azimuth/elevation/head rotation for authorized listener/headset, with acceptable channel interpolation? Do room-wall-door geometry, acoustic materials and moving portals produce perceptually convincing occlusion, transmission, reflections/room decay without WORLD-03 streaming stalls or second physics world? Is CPU/headless fallback consistent even if GPU ray simulation is unavailable?
