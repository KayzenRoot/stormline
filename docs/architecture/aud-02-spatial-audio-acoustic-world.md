## 1. Product acceptance and original design
Catalog acceptance: **"Cenário de posições/obstáculos com áudio validado e custos medidos."** Design spatial audio appropriate for 2D, 2.5D, 3D, Web/native Win/Linux/macOS and CPU/headless: perceptually stable headphone HRTF or speaker panning, correct attenuation/directivity/orientation, source motion and Doppler, geometry-aware obstruction/transmission and material response, early reflections, room-specific late reverberation, path/portal routing and Ambisonics as conditional tiers. There must be a **measured original scene** of multiple sources around doors/walls/rooms and recorded CPU/latency/subjective localization before anyone claims the acceptance has passed. Proposed AAA-quality is a research goal, not proven superiority to any commercial engine.

**Ownership:** AUD-02 owns spatial semantics, acoustic world representation, listener/source environment sample, offline acoustic baking recipes and (candidate) bounded HRTF/panning/propagation renderer effects. AUD-01 alone owns PCM device, hardware callback, voice allocation, buses and mixing; AUD-03 owns reusable generic DSP graph/effect-node scheduling/quality tier; AUD-04 owns music/dialogue narrative cues. No second device/mixer in Steam Audio/OpenAL/other library; adopt only a private adapter or constrained subset after tests. No spatial algorithm may decide whether a GAME actor is in range, takes damage or is visible: game and physics state remain authoritative.

## 3. High-level processing and CPU/headless schedule
```text
WORLD-02 entity/listener pose + WORLD-03 immutable resident Cell/Origin snapshot
  -> source/room/portal profile and validated acoustic material revision
  -> CORE-03 bounded async direct occlusion/visibility and room/portal queries
  -> optional offline acoustic bake via DATA-05 (IR/probe/pathing manifest)
  -> optional RT ray/path propagation only on bounded non-audio workers
  -> spatial acoustic sample [world+cell+source+listener+geometry epochs]
  -> smooth interpolation/doppler/attenuation + HRTF/panner/early/late effect plan
  -> preallocated immutable `AcousticRenderPlanV1` checked at audio quantum boundary
  -> AUD-01 callback consumes fixed bounded DSP work to produce PCM
  -> AUD-01 own voice/bus/mix and audio output; no ray scene reads or file I/O in callback
```
**Important scheduling proof:** direct HRTF convolution may be performed in the audio callback **only** after exact bounded, preinitialized kernel and CPU worst-case proof; HRTF table load/filter design, FFT planning, IR graph rebuild, mesh raycasts, geometry cooking, probe interpolation setup and source file decodes are off RT. Heavy convolution reverb should be AUD-03 DSP kernel with AUD-02 supplied bounded IR/profile and staged snapshots; if future AUD-03 is absent, fall back to simple preallocated approximation or a room-gain/delay send, not import large arbitrary convolution into AUD-01. No sample-rate resampling twice.

## 4. Spatial coordinates, audio timing and source types
`AcousticSourceV1` and `ListenerPoseV1` use WorldId, EntityGuid/generation, TransformRevision, OriginEpoch, orientation quaternion, velocity and source directivity axis. Positions in CORE-05 world units converted once to listener-relative meters; handedness, forward/up axis, azimuth/elevation conventions and stereo output channel order frozen by typed contract. Unloaded WORLD cell never silently becomes a known empty acoustic field.
Source modes: POINT (mono point source), EXTENDED (capsule/area/multi-point with bounded geometry), AMBISONIC_BED (decoded orientation and channel order explicitly tagged), SCREEN_SPACE_2D (UI or 2D game with no fictitious height/depth), 2_5D (world plane + explicit depth/occlusion and camera parallax; never infer acoustics from painted pixel art), and HEAD_LOCKED (non-spatial/accessibility). Multiple listeners require independent per-listener mix/interests or a selected active listener policy; **never** sum all avatars' hearing into one server-truth audio.
`AcousticSampleTimestampV1` anchors CORE-04 TickId, AudioFrameId and DeviceEpoch as mapping from AUD-01 with declared latency/staleness and smoothing. HRTF update cadence/occlusion query cadence are independent and bounded; under variable rendering FPS, spatial audio remains clocked to samples, not graphics frames. Doppler computed from **radial** source/listener relative velocity and declared speed of sound, bounded to prevent wild pitch; speed-of-sound, atmosphere/water media, game assists require reproducible authored policy. Supersonic edge cases do not create impossible/unsafe infinite pitch.

## 5. Binaural/HRTF, speaker and accessibility tiers
- **Baseline low-tier:** pan/gain (mono to stereo or speaker-layout tagged panner) with explicit distance attenuation and directed source cone. No mandatory SOFA, convolution engine, Steam Audio or GPU. Headless deterministically evaluates gain/pan and PCM reference through AUD-01 future NullSink.
- **Headphone HRTF tier:** convolve/direct sound mono with finite impulse response pair encoding direction-dependent ITD/ILD and spectral pinna cues. Interpolation between SOFA measurement directions with stable crossfade; declare HRTF sample rate, coordinate convention and dataset provenance. HRTF is optimized for headphones; **never apply headphone binaural filtering blindly to stereo loudspeakers** with crosstalk. Individual differences mean front/back and elevation are not universally perfect.
- **Speaker mode:** independent speaker layout/gain-panning and downmix mapping, with HRTF disabled unless verified headphone path. Mono fallback preserves audibility and dialogue/UI priority; player option for binaural/spatial reduction. Blindfold listening task and accessibility rubric future, not presumed.
- **Ambisonics:** typed order/normalization/ACN/SN3D vs FuMa, orientation and output decoder; budget/rights/dataset validation needed. Higher orders and head tracking conditional per platform and GPU/CPU tier, not mandatory baseline.
- **HRTF datasets:** AES SOFA is a file standard, **NOT automatic copyright permission**. libmysofa may load official SOFA formats (codec limits) but every embedded/default/user personalized dataset needs separate title/author/license and privacy/capability review. Personalized ear/head measurements may be sensitive biometric-like data and require explicit opt-in, local storage and deletion/export rules.

## 6. Direct sound: attenuation, air absorption, directivity and occlusion
Attenuation policy: finite near radius, authored distance curve (monotonic bounded log/inverse/custom), far-radius virtualization signal to AUD-01 (no unsanctioned voice stealing) and air attenuation per frequency band when sourced. Emitters may be directional (simple cone/spherical harmonics) and near-field extended source approximation with bounded samples.
**Occlusion:** use WORLD-02 geometry source explicitly cooked to acoustic triangles/proxies and optionally SIM-02 same-epoch collision query where suitable. Define single ray or bounded multi-ray coverage, physical materials absorption/transmission coefficients by 3-band octave-like profile (strict [0,1], not magic full realism); combine glass, walls, doors, porous materials and thickness only within model validity. Never equate mesh invisibility/render LoD with air transparency or wall collider with perfect acoustics. Unknown, unloaded or stale geometry yields `GEOMETRY_UNREADY`/uncertainty with **last-verified safe acoustic plan + timed fallback**; avoid knowingly treating missing wall as absent. Truth of GAME occlusion/stealth is separate and server-authorized.
**Moving walls/doors:** world source epoch and portal revision invalidation controls open/closed interpolation. Structural destruction SIM-06 may invalidate acoustic bake or room graph, but cannot make instant listener leak through wall before new source accepted. No data race with PHYS streaming and audio quantum.

## 7. Rooms, reverb, reflections, transmission, diffraction and portals
Original `AcousticRoomGraphV1`: room/zone IDs with WORLD EntityGuid/source hash and global boundaries; portals between zones have staged open fraction, material, area, loss curve and topology revision. Room/portal graph is a derived acoustic view from WORLD scene, not a second world/level DB. `ReflectionPlan`: early delay taps with path lengths and frequency-dependent attenuation plus random diffuse tail estimate; `LateReverbPlan` provides bus send to AUD-03 effect, or simpler fallback when unavailable. Multiple scene rooms/volumes use hysteresis, room crossfade and perceptual continuity; no sudden audible slam on crossing seam unless an authored door close says so.
**Physics-based propagation tier**: ray/segment tracing with energy loss and bounce limits on CORE-03 jobs, optionally offline probe paths/IR bakes, not gameplay tick or audio callback. Must bound number of active sources/listeners, dynamic triangles/portals, per-source rays, reflections/path order, impulse response length, IR bank bytes, CPU frame time, memory, shared jobs, streaming cost, and async pending tasks. Geometry acceleration like Embree is an **optional CPU ray intersection comparator**, not a complete wave solver. Geometric ray tracing is an approximation, especially for diffraction/low frequency/wave effects; call out the approximation.
Dynamic objects occupy a separate fast update lane with depth/occlusion, slow room propagation recalculation, and deterministic publish when source/room revisions agree. Heavy GPU acoustic ray/IR calculations optional ONLY if GPU availability, memory and readback/fence tested; CPU fallback/headless always works with reduced quality. No GPU-computed gameplay sound detection.

## 8. Baking and streaming acoustic content
`AcousticCookRecipeV1` collects immutable WORLD-02 source revisions, acoustic-only geometry/proxy material assignments, room/portal mesh, authored listener/source probes, IR sample rate and max duration, seed, library/toolchain version and target platform capability. DATA-03 validates source mesh/rights/SOFA/IR inputs; DATA-05 outputs verified chunks/bundles with hashes, quality tier, resident cell bounds, probe grid/IR bank metadata, source manifest and migration policy. **AUD-02 never edits the authoritative scene or privately writes a bundle**.
WORLD-03 leases acoustic cells, publishes `AcousticGeometryEpoch` after matching World/Cell/Origin source versions, prefetches ahead of listener route; cancel stale jobs and use bounded last verified fallback when cells unload. A baked IR for obsolete mesh/door/destruction source cannot be silently reused as current; mark STALE and crossfade/fallback or rebuild. Cross-cell sound path must not claim an open acoustic portal when one of its source cells is unknown. Asset licensing included for computed IR if derived from protected HRTF/source. Editor/CLI/MCP all use one authenticated authored profile transaction and preview source manifest, not local shell execution.

## 9. Safety, privacy and runtime reliability
Adversarial mesh triangles and topological holes, SOFA/HDF5 decompression attacks and malformed metadata, enormous IR/time span, unauthorized data uploads, hidden enemy sources leaked via acoustic query, remote MCP geometry+perception queries, infinite reflection ray budget, stale callback filter coeff pointers, cache eviction while RT processing, and high RMS/gain hearing discomfort are high-risk *future* issues. Propose strict schema size/range and source-right check at DATA-03; sandbox/untrusted file import; safe integer arithmetic; bounded rays/segments/IR taps/bytes; generation/epoch reference safety and deferred free; deny private/NET-ineligible acoustic source inspection. Never run untrusted HRTF parser, acoustic geometry import, or solver on callback. AUDIO outputs should not expose precise hidden actor positions to server-unapproved clients. Optional personalized HRTF requires explicit consent and retention policy; no microphone, camera or head-scan permissions requested automatically.
If an acoustic job misses deadline, degrade explicitly: from simulated early/late reverb -> last confirmed room response -> bounded occlusion + HRTF -> distance/pan; never hang or corrupt AUD-01. Protect critical dialogue and accessibility cues per AUD-01 priority; attenuate occlusion/reverb conservatively and user-tunable clarity.
