## 2. World, dimensions and units
`PhysicsWorld2DIdV1` scoped to ProjectId, WorldId, WorldInstanceEpoch and a fixed `PhysicalScaleV1` meters-per-world-unit or pixels-per-meter conversion at the TWO-01 boundary. Physics internal meters, floats/doubles/numeric policy pinned and checked; renderer pixels are view-space only. Define gravity in m/s², velocity m/s, acceleration m/s², angular rad/s, angle rad, force N, torque N·m, mass kg and density kg/m² for 2D solver (2D mass/density semantics backend-specific need adapter); no implicit pixels-as-meters. Non-finite and absurd scales fail before mutation, numerical near-zero tolerance versioned/explicit.

- 2D world plane and handedness/y-axis contract must be explicit: screen Y-down canvas requires documented conversion to physics Y-up (candidate), and source scene/2.5D can embed in a 3D camera without accidentally promoting 3D contact normals. No silent rotation/scale sign inversions. 2D physics layer/order is unrelated to visual draw layer.
- Three body modes `STATIC`, `DYNAMIC`, `KINEMATIC`; sleep/awake and enabled flags; dynamic body mass/inertia derived from attached shapes and density with explicit override policy; zero/negative mass and scale rules checked. Static tiles/chains may be chunk-owned; kinematic platforms provide velocity/target at fixed-tick boundaries rather than renderer frame position deltas.
- World origin and global position compatibility CORE-05 / WORLD-03: choose local physics island with typed `PhysicsOriginEpoch`, rebasing at safe tick barriers with world/scene/PHYS/GFX ACK; no loss of durable EntityGuid or global tile CellId, no invariant requiring float solver stable across infinite coordinates. Large-world precision thresholds and multi-observer scope TBD numeric DoD.

## 3. Engine-owned identity and mutable handle rules
- Public handles `PhysicsBodyHandleV1`, `PhysicsShapeHandleV1`, `PhysicsJointHandleV1` are opaque world+slot+generation with explicit invalid/stale/other-world errors, not Box2D IDs or raw pointers, separate from WORLD-01 `EntityGuid`. Map each body to an optional WORLD-01 qualified EntityGuid and source shape AssetId; multiple colliders on one body allowed, nested prefab instances map through WORLD-02 stable source IDs.
- Queue `PhysicsCommandV1` with `ExpectedTick`, optional expected source revision, WorldInstanceEpoch and actor permissions; only apply at documented pre-step/post-step barriers. Do not destroy body/joint/change filter/teleport while solver step is executing. Validate references/generations on every event read; callbacks may be multithreaded and cannot directly mutate ECS or invoke gameplay scripts. Commands sorted/processed by deterministic admission order when replay policy enabled.
- Body state accepted from authoritative fixed-tick physics or explicitly controlled kinematic inputs. Source ECS transform sync policy `PHYSICS_DRIVES_DYNAMIC`, `AUTHORITATIVE_KINEMATIC_TARGET`, `STATIC_AUTHORING_REVISION` and `RENDER_INTERPOLATED_READONLY`. Parent transform and nonuniform scaling are not silently applied to live collision shapes: require rebake at source-safe barrier with contact invalidation. Negative scale/mirror and parent reparent preserving world position must use CORE-05 checked affine semantics.
- `PhysicsStepReceiptV1` contains tick/revision/WorldEpoch/physics config hash, body counts, events generation and status; ECS publish after solver step at single consistent snapshot barrier. No memory access to event pairs after their step lifetime/owner release.

## 4. Shapes, broadphase, contacts and joints
- Shape source `CIRCLE`, `CAPSULE`, `BOX/ROUNDED_BOX`, `CONVEX_POLYGON`, `SEGMENT/CHAIN` for static terrain, and `COMPOUND` as multiple shapes on a body (backend-supported rules). Convex polygon max vertices and winding, nonself-intersection, finite sizes, positive radius and density, scaling re-cook and chain ghost vertices are validated. Concave dynamic shapes must be decomposed offline via DATA-03/05 or rejected, never arbitrary malicious vertices sent to solver; advanced deformable bodies/ragdoll belong future.
- Contacts broadphase dynamic AABB tree belongs SIM-01 actual collision world, **not** WORLD-03 streaming interest or GFX-04 visibility. Narrowphase contact manifold with penetration tolerances, friction/resting impulses, restitution, warm start/solver iterations and sleeping options pinned per backend; queries `RAY_CAST`, `OVERLAP`, `SHAPE_CAST` and point/hit filter require bounded sort/limits and truthful completeness. Broadphase candidate shapes can generate false positives, final query must refine to exact tested geometry.
- Contact begin/end/hit and sensor enter/leave are post-step immutable `PhysicsEventBatchV1` with World+Tick, shape/body generations, pair identity, point(s) normal/impulse and `SENSOR`/contact flags. Subscription must declare event enablement (performance), event order policy and effect of destroyed shapes/filter changes; delayed events on following step must not be mistaken for current tick. Sensor overlaps **not continuous guarantees for two fast-moving moving sensors**, explicit ray/cast needed in high-speed gameplay. Queue delivery to GAME/AUDIO AFTER physics tick with no arbitrary immediate callback mutating solver.
- Filters `CategoryBits`, `MaskBits`, optional group index and gameplay layer mapping typed, avoid numeric mismatch with render layer. Collision pair `CanCollide(A,B)` must be symmetric as defined by backend; sensors vs solidity and joint `collideConnected` explicit, cross-world forbidden. Filter edits invalidate/rebuild contact pair with typed begin/end receipts and tick consistency.
- Joints baseline `DISTANCE`, `REVOLUTE`, `PRISMATIC`, `WELD`, `MOTOR`, `SPRING` subject to backend feature audit; pulley/wheel/gear are IMPORTANT not automatic. Limits/motor force, anchors, broken body retirement and stress test under chains; exact capabilities defer to backend pinned release/1.0 DoD, no fake support of all joint shapes. Breaking impulse/torque and spring parameter definition must be explicitly versioned.
- CCD: swept collision policies `DISCRETE`, `BULLET/CONTINUOUS_CANDIDATE`, `CAST_REQUIRED`; Box2D bullet and speculative contacts mitigate tunneling but cannot guarantee every dynamic-sensor pair; benchmark high-speed fast-vs-static and fast-vs-dynamic, thin tile corners and one-way edges. For kinematic high-speed triggers, require explicit shape cast and bounded time-of-impact result rather than untested event assertion.

## 5. Fixed timestep, deterministic scope and rollback
- CORE-06 owns fixed-timestep clock/accumulator, tick ID, maximum catch-up ticks, pause/resume/time scaling. Candidate simulation `dt=1/60 s` with explicit substeps (e.g. 4) as a research baseline **not an approved numeric target**. Step only on fixed tick, never use presentation frame `deltaTime` to alter physics outcomes; render interpolates `alpha` from complete previous/current snapshots without feeding interpolated positions back into solver.
- `PhysicsDeterminismProfileV1` locks backend name/version/build/compiler flags/FMA+precision/SIMD, float/rounding policy, body+shape IDs and creation order, stable command/admission ordering, fixed substeps, contact event buffering and thresholds. Official Box2D 3.1+ FAQ claims cross-platform determinism and deterministic multithreading for **same input and single precision**, which is a vendor claim not proof of Stormline and doesn't validate arbitrary different solver backends. Rapier optional enhanced-determinism feature has its own flags, ABI and Rust/WASM cost.
- Snapshot/rollback optional extension `PhysicsSnapshotV1`: state serialization must include bodies, asleep state, joints/contacts/solver warm-start cache or record backend-defined supported state. Merely saving transforms is NOT a faithful rollback. Tests should compare authoritative replay against frozen golden full-state hash and event receipts; capture version and endianness, rehydrate exact world/physics source+tick+backend profile, invalidate old handles or produce versioned remap and forbid stale event pointers. NET decides if deterministic lockstep/prediction is released.
- External/side-effect GAME contact handlers enqueue **next-step** commands with stable ordering; physics replay alone does not guarantee gameplay logic determinism, network authority or audio. Same inputs+same backend profile and exact supported targets required for bit-identical result, not unconditional “physics deterministic everywhere.”

## 6. Tilemaps, streaming and 2.5D integration
- TWO-02 tiles source collision polygons/chains cooked by DATA-05. Avoid one dynamic box collider per tile; combine monotonic chain/edge segments with adjacent ghost vertices to avoid snagging on internal corners; validate winding/valid ghost points and chain bounds. Sliding and step/jump character behavior belongs SIM-03, not SIM-01 solver guarantee.
- Tile/scene cell load from WORLD-03 is `CPU_READY -> PHYS_STAGE_SHAPES -> PRE_STEP_COMMIT -> PHYS_ACK_COLLIDER_READY -> WORLD03 ACTIVE_COLLISION`; no gameplay traversal into nonresident colliders. Unload must wait for live body/contacts/joint pins and safe tick + owner leases; physics bodies crossing cell boundaries have one canonical owner and stable EntityGuid, not recreated on every render/stream cell transition.
- 2D physics plane with 2.5D sorting: visible front/back layers and sprite parallax cannot alter physical layer/filter, source clip/render clipping is independent. Source transforms/sprite scale convert at adapter boundary, no gameplay influenced by camera DPI or canvas zoom.

## 7. Platform, security and testability
- C17 Box2D 3.2.x leading candidate MIT, Chipmunk2D MIT C99 adapter, Rapier2D Apache-2.0 Rust/WASM/FFI alternative; all **NOT ADOPTED**. Potential portability costs (C↔C++ wraps, Rust FFI, app binary/wasm size, ABI, SIMD flags) measured in real Win/Linux/macOS+WASM and headless. No mandatory GPU, 3D physics or paid library.
- Graph/input safety: reject untrusted malformed shape data, nonfinite/huge positions, zero/negative radii or densities, self-crossing chain/vertex winding/degenerate polygon, enormous dynamic bodies/joints, event subscription flood, hostile queries and cross-world references, script callback mutation while stepping, unauthorized user source shape edits, unlicensed sample meshes. Limit per-world body+joint+shape count, source import bytes, query pagination, contact event cap, impulse/velocities, substep/catchup and memory pressure. Fail explicit `PHYSICS_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`, `SIMULATION_NOT_ADVANCED`, never OOM/delete critical ground. Exact budgets TBD owner.
- Headless oracle fixtures: falling circles/boxes, resting towers, stacks/piles, cradle/joint chain, concave terrain chain/tiles, kinematic platform, fast bullet, bidirectional sensors, multilayer mask pairs, 2.5D visual decoupling, teleport/rollback with stale handles and WORLD-03 source/collision readiness. Compare independent analytic invariants where valid (energy not strictly conserved with friction), broadphase spatial oracle and golden replay state hash, not graphic FPS. Real CPU fixed tick p50/p95/p99, peak memory and native/Web results required later.
- Event callbacks poststep and fixed-tick owner boundaries prevent data races. Use TSan/ASan/UBSan where supported and fuzz imported polygon chains/serialized state. No source mutation or simulation achieved in P00.
