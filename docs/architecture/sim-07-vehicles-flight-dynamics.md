## 1. Product intent and honest acceptance
Support drivable cars/trucks, tanks/tracked vehicles, boats and aircraft/rotorcraft inside original 2D/2.5D/3D Stormline games with a headless authoritative-server path and native Windows/Linux/macOS/Web capability profiles. Catalog acceptance: **"Original drivable tank, truck and aircraft/rotorcraft reference tests with reproducible physics and network timing."** That requires playable original reference scenes, actual build/physics behavior, recorded network timing and numerical tolerance; research alone satisfies none of it.

Vehicle Core is **Stormline-owned typed control/configuration/state semantics**, not a second physics engine. Physics integration, contact truth, constraints and scene transforms are exclusively owned by SIM-02. Per-module vehicles produce forces/torques/contact requests at approved SIM-02 safe barriers and read immutable physics receipts. GAME-01 controls device remapping/action intent, GAME authority decides seat/ability permissions, SIM-03 owns character enter/exit and collision state, NET-02/03/04 eventually own server snapshots/prediction/reconciliation. Never introduce an independent timestamp, body simulator, renderer or gameplay damage rules.

## 3. Shared model, typed profiles and modes
Versioned `VehicleAssetV1` references owned source asset, immutable hash, mass/inertia/bounds/collision profile, `DriveDomain` (WHEELED/TRACKED/WATERCRAFT/FIXED_WING/ROTORCRAFT/2D_ARCADE), control surfaces, force producers, explicit propulsion engine+powertrain, authored seat/occupant anchors, lights/FX/audio asset IDs, profile revision, author rights and target capability tier. `VehicleInstanceV1` uses Project/World/ECS/PHYS epochs and generational handles. `VehicleControlIntentV1` is source-actor authorized and ordered by CORE-04 TickId; state/force/telemetry/error receipts immutable and paginated.
Shared state includes BodyHandle, angular/linear velocities, orientation, validated terrain/water/wind sample refs, gear/engine RPM, fuel/energy (GAME-owned resource when relevant), wheel/track/buoyancy/wing/rotor stage status and active control mode. Never imply realistic physical energy conservation when gameplay assistance is active: label `SIMULATION` vs `ASSISTED_ARCADE` authority modes and capture assists in replay.
A vehicle may be player-driven, AI-driven or scripted **only through GAME-authorized controls**. Seat lifecycle `OUTSIDE → BOARDING_PENDING → OCCUPIED → EXIT_PENDING → OUTSIDE` is coordinated at safe SIM-03/PHYS barrier, checks seat ownership/permissions, occupancy conflict, character collider and clearance, vehicle authority epoch and rollback of failed boarding. Remote/client predicted camera/interpolation does not teleport motor/physics.

## 4. Ground wheeled vehicles: dynamics and tires
First future NECESSARY proof: simple 4-wheel car/truck on original road, one BodyHandle with suspension ray or shape query per wheel, bounded spring-damper force along suspension axis, traction/longitudinal vs lateral slip curves, basic drivetrain torque/gear/differential/brakes/handbrake, steering geometry and optional stability controls. Wheel-to-chassis impulses must be SIM-02 requests and contact ACK; steering/wheel mesh rotations GFX-only. `VehicleConstraint` and `WheeledVehicleController` of Jolt are **leading candidates**, not public ABI nor adopted. Candidate reference exact tire equations from carefully documented sources with validity envelope, no invented "perfect Pacejka" full model; tire relaxation, load sensitivity, thermal wear, ABS/ESP and full racing simulation are IMPORTANT/FUTURE with proof.
Contact material/wetness/terrain source and load transfer must be sampled using SIM-02 collider/material revision; friction coeff finite with explicit unit. Fast-moving wheel shape casts, stairs/curbs, moving platforms, high chassis and airborne/no-ground cases require typed UNREADY/unsupported response; never infer contact from graphics terrain. Off-road drivetrain/wheel traction can be aggregated; no real-time granular soil model is NECESSARY.

## 5. Tanks/tracked vehicles
Track profile: independently controlled left/right effective track speed/drive torque/braking ratio, ground contact along multiple road-wheel/suspension points, contact patches and terrain traction, yaw/differential steering, bound slip/energy and track tension **presentation only** unless future explicit track-chain simulation. Jolt `TrackedVehicleController` is a leading reference under SIM-02 adapter; Chrono::Vehicle tracked system is high-fidelity comparator (cannot run a second physics world inside SIM-02). A turret is a GAME-validated actuator constrained by SIM-02; weapons, projectiles and damage stay GAME/NET, not SIM-07. Corrupted track, lost segment/physics event or terrain crush after SIM-06 destruction triggers safe downgrade, traction re-sampling and source epoch validation. Tracks must not create infinite tangential impulse or uncontrolled yaw at zero speed.

## 6. Boats and water interaction
Original buoyancy baseline: validated sampled flotation points/voxelized volume and world-water interface via WORLD-04; hydrostatic force from *submerged displaced volume*, bounded drag, damping and directional hull resistance; optional propeller/jet motor force, rudder moment and wave-surface response. Hydrostatic unit checks density kg/m^3, gravity m/s^2, displaced m^3, force N; never apply area as volume. Water surface source `WaterSampleReceiptV1` includes cell/residency, time/wave revision, current velocity and completeness; render waves must not silently be authoritative collision water. If water cell unloaded, never declare floating support from stale height; pause/mark incomplete or choose explicit last-safe physically consistent mode.
Wave current/boats moving through multi-cell currents require tested motion, caps and slamming response. Sailing rigs, planing hull hydrodynamics, foils, 6DoF fluid-structure CFD and multi-boat wakes are FUTURE and expensive, not implicit baseline. Boats never run a second fluid solver; optional water sampling/flow source is owned by WORLD/physics contract. Roll/pitch stable handling and reverse propeller validated in future native/headless replay.

## 7. Fixed-wing flight
Two coherent candidates:
A. **Stormline owned real-time 6DoF force model:** from validated aircraft profile with wing/control-surface areas, chord/center-of-pressure, density/altitude/wind, body velocity, angle of attack/sideslip and bounded coefficient curves `CL(alpha)`, `CD(alpha)`, `CY(beta)`, pitch/roll/yaw moments. Compute aerodynamic forces/torques at sample points, engine thrust/torque and landing gear contact, request to SIM-02 at fixed tick. Gravity and body integration stay SIM-02. Nonfinite airspeed, stall reversal, zero density and near-zero speed have explicit controlled handling, no negative mass.
B. **JSBSim** is a mature 6DoF Flight Dynamics Model, **LGPL-2.1** with independent state integration and XML-configurable property engine. It is a reference/optional adapter only, **NOT a drop-in force model without a single-authority integration protocol**. Before selection, choose whether JSBSim owns full flight integration and SIM-02 mirrors collision under an explicit validated coupling, or use derived coefficients in an owned force-only model. Running JSBSim's full integrator plus SIM-02 integrator on same body is **rejected**. LGPL link/distribution obligations and XML sandboxing must be reviewed with legal/packaging and NLE-D009 engine license.
Aerobatics, ground effect, compressibility, icing/weather turbulence, damage flight envelope and autopilots are staged IMPORTANT/FUTURE until reference/oracle. UI must not claim aviation-certified physics or real aircraft operational accuracy.

## 8. Rotorcraft and vertical flight
Proposed bounded `RotorProfileV1`: mass/CG, rotor plane/diameter/blade count, effective lift/drag, collective/cyclic/yaw controls, rotor RPM/spool and engine torque, tail rotor or coaxial anti-torque, translational lift and ground effect where validated. First future playable prototype uses an **explicit simplified actuator-disc/force-and-moment model** with full 6DoF SIM-02 body coupling; no unbounded blade-element solver in low-tier baseline. Rotor thrust direction and gyroscopic/torque response tied to physical chassis orientation and validated unit frames; landing gear/surface contacts SIM-02 only. No physically impossible mode switch without player-visible assist classification. Vortex ring, retreating-blade stall, autorotation, downwash/wake-fluid interaction, wind shear and detailed blade-element momentum are FUTURE per measured need; preserve realistic failure cases as future proof obligations instead of false current claim.

## 9. Control, AI and network timing
`GAME-01` maps player keyboard/gamepad/touch to normalized, rate-limited action intents (throttle, brake, steering, shift, clutch, tracks-left/right, trim, flap, elevator/aileron/rudder, collective/cyclic/yaw). All controls require ActorId, VehicleGeneration, AuthorityEpoch, SeatId, TickId, producer sequence, expected profile revision and finite analog range; server/GAME validates seat occupant/permissions and anti-cheat speed/force. Input smoothing/rate limiting and accessibility/rebinding belong GAME-01; SIM-07 receives **normalized validated control**, calculates bounded force/actuator requests and consumes SIM-02 PHYS ACK. AI autopilot/pilot/boat route follows GAME/SIM-05 authorized control without direct BodyHandle write.
**Proposed schedule:** confirmed WORLD-03/PHYS ground/water/wind state (tick t-1 or staged snapshot) → authorized controls tick t → vehicle force derivation → SIM-02 force/constraint application before physics step → SIM-02 confirmed VehiclePhysicsReceipt → publish vehicle telemetry and event intents → NET/GFX/AUD read-only snapshots. Actual barrier contract must be frozen with CORE-04 and SIM-02 before code. Important: do not require AI/SIM-03 transition and PHYS to consume each other's *same tick* post-step output cyclically; prefer explicit next-tick queues.
NET-02 authoritative headless state, NET-03 quantized snapshot/interests, NET-04 prediction/correction with bounded rollback and mismatch telemetry. Log input ordering, tick rate, random seed if used, profile/backend/terrain/wind versions; stale remote commands and packet replay are rejected. **Cross-platform bit-identical dynamics, lag robustness and 64-client perf are NOT PROVEN**.

## 10. Source integrity, security and recoverability
Untrusted vehicle XML/config, gigantic aircraft surface table, fanout of rotor/wheel suspension, NaN coefficients, invalid inertia, negative area, impulse/torque overflow, stale terrain/water source, moving world origin and race during seat/entity detach are future HIGH risks. Validate zero/positive mass/inertia eigenvalues, bounded lookup tables, stable interpolation/extrapolation policy, finite torque/velocity and maximum suspension/rest geometry; enforce per-vehicle CPU/jobs, point counts, constraints, network intents/s and telemetry quota. DATA-03 importer and DATA-05 cooker enforce file rights, no external URL/XXE/executable script, authenticated source and schema revision. Save or hot reload only at staged DATA-04/WORLD/SIM-02 barrier with explicit cancellation and rollback/roll-forward. No runtime tools can send unbounded torque, force or remote shell commands.
No vehicle models from real games or proprietary telemetry datasets included. Third-party vehicle/aircraft sample content needs separate rights/attribution review even when a library source is permissive. Simulation is entertainment/research, not validated for real flight, road/autopilot or maritime safety.
