## 3. CD-02 — Affinity limitation

The helper still creates the process with `System.Diagnostics.Process.Start`, then applies `ProcessorAffinity` and reads the effective mask back. The readback proves the mask at that point, not from the first process instruction. The first startup instructions may run on the default mask before assignment. This is recorded as:

```text
affinity_coverage=post_start_readback
```

The historical and future measurements under this helper are **approximate affinity profiles**, not proof that affinity was active from process creation. A suspended-process/`CreateProcess` + `SetProcessAffinityMask` + `ResumeThread` implementation was not introduced because it requires substantially more native/PowerShell plumbing and was not necessary to keep the correction proportional; the limitation is explicit and measurements remain report-only.
